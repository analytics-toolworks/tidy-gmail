# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

## Your Gmail Account Belongs to You

`tidy-gmail` is a free, open-source application
that helps you review, organize, and clean up
your Gmail account.

**You are the only user** of your `tidy-gmail` installation.
The developer of `tidy-gmail` does not have access
to your Gmail account, messages, contacts,
authorization credentials, or cleanup decisions.

`tidy-gmail` runs locally on your computer.
It is not a hosted email management service.

## Your Own Google Authorization

Each user creates and manages their own Google Cloud project and OAuth credentials.

- You authorize `tidy-gmail` directly through Google.
- Your authorization credentials and tokens are stored locally on your computer.
- The developer does not receive your OAuth tokens or Gmail credentials.
- You can revoke access at any time through your Google Account settings.

No shared developer-owned OAuth client is required.

## Gmail Data Access

`tidy-gmail` requests the Gmail `gmail.modify`
permission to support its features, including:

- Inspecting messages and sender information.
- Identifying potential cleanup candidates.
- Producing reports for your review.
- Moving approved messages to Trash.
- Restoring messages when supported by the application.

Scanning and reporting do not modify Gmail messages.
Cleanup operations are subject to the application's
configuration and safeguards.

## Local Data Storage

`tidy-gmail` stores its configuration, authorization information,
sender decisions, reports, and cleanup records
locally in your workspace.

The developer has no access to this information.

You control your local files, backups, and access permissions.
Protect your workspace and OAuth credentials
as you would other sensitive personal information.

## No Developer Access or Data Sharing

`tidy-gmail` does not transmit your Gmail messages,
account contents, OAuth credentials, or
cleanup records to its developer.

Your Gmail data is not sold, shared with advertisers,
used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Communication with Google's Gmail API is necessary
to perform operations you authorize.
Google's handling of information
is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You control the locally stored data created by `tidy-gmail`.

You may delete local reports, configuration files, and
saved authorization tokens.
You may also revoke `tidy-gmail`'s access in your Google Account.

Deleting local files does not automatically reverse changes
already made to Gmail.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

`tidy-gmail`'s use of information received from Google APIs
is intended to comply with the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including its Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is accessed
only to provide the application's user-directed
Gmail management functionality.

## Open Source

`tidy-gmail` is open source.
You can inspect the code to understand how
Gmail authorization, local processing, and cleanup operations work.

**Your Gmail account is yours.**
Your `tidy-gmail` installation is always under your control.
