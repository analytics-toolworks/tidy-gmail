**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

## About The App

`tidy-gmail` is a free, open-source,
locally operated Gmail management tool.

It allows individuals to inspect, organize, and
clean up their own Gmail accounts
using Google's Gmail API.

## Your Installation, Your Control

Each user installs and operates
their own instance of `tidy-gmail`.

You provide your own Google Cloud OAuth credentials and
authorize access to your own Gmail account.

The developer **does not** operate your instance,
manage your Gmail account, or
have access to your authorized Gmail data.

## Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- Authorizing access only to Gmail accounts you are permitted to manage.
- Reviewing cleanup candidates and configuration settings.
- Protecting your local credentials, tokens, and reports.
- Maintaining backups or recovery records appropriate to your needs.
- Reviewing the results of operations that modify your Gmail account.

## Gmail Operations

`tidy-gmail` supports inspection and reporting
as well as optional message cleanup and restoration operations.

Some operations can modify your Gmail account.
You are responsible for reviewing and approving
the actions you choose to perform.

Google's own policies, permissions, and
service limitations also apply.
